章法Rules at work

  • Brush art lives only in the poster; every control below it uses the line-icon voice.
  • The sun disc is washed to 36% and slides off the corner; it still carries no text.
  • One coffee block per screen owns the money moment: the cart bar with total and checkout.
  • Steppers appear only for items already in the cart; everything else keeps the single round add.
  • No seal on this screen: the poster caption does the branding, and two is the ceiling anyway.
