SproutUI kit

Every control on this board is a physical prop: buttons are baked biscuits, sliders are wooden planks, section headers are painted signs. Everything is drawn on a 3px pixel grid - no smooth curve anywhere on the page.

Foundations

Ten pinned colors and one pixel unit. Click a seed packet to copy its hex. The four cocoa shades below the packets are derived - bevels for dark pieces plus two verified text inks.

Body copy is a plain system sans so paragraphs stay easy on the eyes; the pixel voice is saved for labels and signs. On green grounds body ink darkens one step to keep its footing.

Construction

No border-radius on this page. Corners are clip-path staircases stepping two pixel-units in; edges are one-unit crust layers; depth is a one-unit bevel - light on top, dark below. Press a biscuit and the bevel goes away while the label drops exactly one unit.

Field notes

Every crop, price, and stock level on this sheet is synthetic demo data from an imaginary farm. Sprout is an exploration in the UIplayground workspace - an original CSS/SVG homage to the Sprout Lands UI asset pack by Cup Nooble. The style is recreated from scratch; no assets were copied.
