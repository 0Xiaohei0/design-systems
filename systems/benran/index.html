素材 Foundations

本然 - benran, "what is so of itself" - is the stationery identity of a small tea atelier. Every surface begins as empty paper; the work is deciding what little may enter. Objects are solid ink and rest on a hairline shelf, words stand upright as they do on a scroll, and color is spent the way seal paste is: one stamp at a time.
