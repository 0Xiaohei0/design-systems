/home/enjoy/readme.txt

enjoy is personal computing as a warm hobby. every element is a window or a desk object: 2px ink outlines, cream fills, hard 3px offset shadows, one mono typeface, and exactly one saturated blue field. scroll the desk: foundations, motifs, components, then the player.
