可以 / 不可以 Do / Don't

用光做深度

Depth from light. Same color as the page.

DO 可以

Outlined card

Border, hard shadow, cool gray - everything MOCHI is not.

DON'T 不可以 borders, hard edges, pure gray
  • No outlines anywhere - the 2px --deep focus ring is the single exception.
  • Light always comes from the top-left; never flip a shadow.
  • One dark plate color only: --deep, reserved for the clock face and rare dark plates.
  • White text only on --deep or on deepened pastels at large-bold sizes; small text on pastel uses ink on the light tints.
