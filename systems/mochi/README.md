# MOCHI 麻薯

Soft neumorphic kawaii widget system. The phone as a plush desk toy: one warm
greige material, every widget a raised pillow, depth from light instead of
lines, muted pastel plates, and bilingual (Chinese-first) labels.

Status: **Exploration** - direction pinned by the client from a reference home
screen, 2026-08-03.

## Contents

- `DESIGN.md` - the spec: tokens, the raised/pressed material rule, contrast
  rules, motif vocabulary, component states, do/don't. Read this first.
- `index.html` - the catalog: hero home-screen recreation, foundations,
  material, motifs, components, and a music-player application demo.
- `css/mochi-core.css` - tokens, material primitives, and all components.
- `css/portal-card.css` - the system's card on the root portal (owned by the
  portal task).
