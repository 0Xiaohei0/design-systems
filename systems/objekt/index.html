OBJEKT is a design system in which every interface element is a printed label. Buttons are stamps, values are serials, panels are paper stock, and depth exists only as the hard shadow of a sticker lifting off the sheet. Four stocks and their inks are the entire palette; no accent hue, no gradient, no radius, no blur. This sheet is the catalog: stocks, type voices, motifs, components with every state, and one assembled spec sheet, printed at scale 1:1 for designers picking parts.

Secondary text is always a darker or lighter step of the stock's own ink, never a mid gray: #34312B on the light stocks, #B9B5AA on black. All body ink holds at least 4.5:1 on its stock.
