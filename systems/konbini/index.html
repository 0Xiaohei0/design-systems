the one gradient

Exactly one gradient exists in the whole system: the vertical sky, deep at the top and pale at the horizon. It is always vertical and always a sky. Bands, plates, buttons, and shadows are flat fills.

display voice

A heavy neutral grotesque, 700 to 800, tight tracking, set white and giant when it floats in sky. On sky it is a poster voice only: at least 32px and bold, never body copy.

the pixel rule

ピクセル

All illustration is pixel art on a fixed integer grid: inline SVG made only of axis-aligned rects at integer coordinates, rendered with crisp edges. No curves, no strokes, no anti-aliasing. Scenes are drawn on a 176x104 grid, icons on 16x16.

Scaling happens in whole steps. A pixel is 1x, 2x, 3x, or 4x, and never 1.5x: CSS width is always an integer multiple of the grid, so the pixels stay square and crisp at every size.
